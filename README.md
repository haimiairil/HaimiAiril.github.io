# From Developer to CEO: The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business

**Industry Talk 1 | TIS Webinar**
SECP1513 | Faculty of Computing, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia | Semester 01, 2026/2027

## About the Talk

- **Speaker:** Assoc. Prof. Dr. Hj. Mohamad Hafis Izran bin Ishak, CEO of AR Mechatronics PLT
- **Date:** 26 October 2026, 2:00-4:00 PM
- **Platform:** Cisco Webex

## Executive Summary

The talk highlighted the different visions of technology in system development and business. Developers focus on making the system work, while a CEO prioritises the sustainability of the operation. The speaker also shared his entrepreneurial experience and encouraged the new generation to explore new things.

## Content of the Talk

1. **Nailing the tech basics:** a solid technical foundation is still essential.
2. **Seeing the business side:** build solutions around real user needs and real value.
3. **Communication is everything:** bridge the gap between technical and non-technical people.
4. **Stepping into leadership:** guide people and adapt when things change.

## Key Reflections

- Being an IT professional is not just about technical skills, but also business sustainability.
- The MVP (Minimum Viable Product) approach: release core features early and learn from user feedback.
- The MYRATS case study: successful technology solves real problems for real users.
- AI should be seen as a productivity tool, not a substitute.
